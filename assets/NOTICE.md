# Asset provenance

- `offloop.png`: rasterized from the Offloop product repository's official `frontend/public/logo.svg`, preserving its mark and theme green.
- `openai.png`, `deepseek.png`, `claude.png`: raster versions of the model/provider marks already used in the approved Offloop comparison template, sourced from the product repository's brand assets.
- `gemini.png`: the Gemini sparkle extracted from the repository's Gemini wordmark, preserving the original artwork.
- `hy4.png`: Tencent Hunyuan mark cropped from Tencent's Hy4-preview logo asset at https://huggingface.co/tencent/Hy4-preview/resolve/main/assets/logo-en.png.
- These marks identify the compared providers. Trademark and artwork rights remain with their respective owners; no endorsement is implied and this package does not grant rights to third-party marks.
- `InterVariable.ttf`: Inter font, distributed under the SIL Open Font License. See `Inter-OFL.txt` for the complete license and copyright notice.
- `music.wav`: locally synthesized electronic instrumental created for this Offloop editing workflow. It contains no imported song recording. It is included as the template's reusable soundtrack.
